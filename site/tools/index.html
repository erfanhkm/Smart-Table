Why these calculators show their working

Most restaurant calculators hide the math and borrow an industry average you cannot check. Ours do the opposite: you enter your own numbers, and each page prints its formula and its assumptions in plain sight — including the places where a commonly-cited benchmark range is only a rough reference, not a target for your room. The arithmetic runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved. If you want the thinking behind the payment numbers, start with how much does slow payment cost your restaurant?
