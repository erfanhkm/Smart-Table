How Smart Table Works

Designed for speed from the moment guests sit down.

01

Guest Scans the QR

Each table features a tabletop device. Guests scan the QR code with their phone camera to instantly view their itemized bill.

02

Splits & Pays in Seconds

Guests pay by card in their phone browser — equal splits, split by items, or custom amounts, with tip.

03

Table Frees Up Live

Staff see the status update live on the dashboard the second payment is confirmed.
