How Smart Table works

Built for the end of the meal.

01

Guest scans the QR

Each table gets a QR code — printed from your dashboard today, built into the Smart Table device when it ships. Guests scan it with their phone camera to view their itemized bill.

02

Split & pay by card

Guests pay by card in their phone browser — equal splits, split by items, or custom amounts, with tip.

03

Staff see it paid

The dashboard shows the table as paid within seconds of confirmation — staff free it in one tap.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace our POS?

No — today it works with Square. It reads your open checks and records payments back.

Do guests need an app?

No — everything happens in the phone browser.

What about cash?

Staff record cash payments from the dashboard.

What does it cost?

A device purchase plus a subscription. Founding pricing is set one-on-one — talk to us.

When is it available?

We're onboarding founding restaurants across Metro Vancouver now. The software works with Square today; the tabletop device is planned next, and founding restaurants get first units.
