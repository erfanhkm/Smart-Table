How It Works

Three simple steps. No apps to download, no accounts to create.

01

Guest Scans QR

Every table features a discreet QR code. Guests scan with their phone camera to instantly view their itemized bill.

02

Splits & Pays Instantly

Split equally, by items, or custom amounts. Pay in under 30 seconds by card, right in the phone browser — no app download.

03

Table Frees Up

Staff dashboard updates live instantly as bills settle, turning tables faster and increasing daily revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it replace our POS?

No. Smart Table sits directly on top of your existing Square POS system, linking checks and orders seamlessly without replacing your hardware workflow.

Do guests need to download an app?

Zero friction. Guests simply point their phone camera at the tabletop QR code and pay directly in their mobile browser in under 30 seconds.

Who handles cash payments?

Staff handle cash as usual. Cash payments are recorded instantly in the staff dashboard with a single tap so floor records stay synchronized.

During our current pilot phase, pricing is custom-tailored for independent venues. Contact us to discuss pilot terms.
