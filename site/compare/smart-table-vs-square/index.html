Smart Table vs Square: Two Tools, Two Different Jobs

Most comparisons force a winner. This one should not: Square is your point of sale, and Smart Table is the guest ordering and payment layer that runs on top of it. They are used together, not instead of each other.

By the Smart Table team · November 19, 2026 · 6 min read

Full disclosure: we build Smart Table, and Smart Table only works because Square exists underneath it. So read this page as a guide to what each tool does and what the combination costs — not as a contest. If you came here deciding which to buy, the honest answer for most restaurants already on Square is: keep Square, and add Smart Table only if the end of the meal is where your night slows down.

The short answer

Square runs your side of the counter: orders, kitchen flow, card payments, menus, reports, refunds and payouts. Smart Table runs the guest's side of the last ten minutes: guests scan a QR code, view the live bill from Square, order, split the cheque, tip and pay from their own phone — while staff watch every table from one dashboard. Different jobs, same cheque.

What Square does — and does well

Square is a mature, complete point-of-sale platform, and for many independent restaurants it is genuinely all the staff-side system they need. Its strengths are breadth and polish: order entry at the counter or on handhelds, kitchen display options, online ordering, team management, and add-ons such as loyalty and marketing. Your menu, open cheques and money live there — payments settle to your merchant account, refunds happen there, and your bookkeeper's reports come from there.

Square also sells the hardware around the POS, from readers to registers, with a free point-of-sale tier alongside paid plans — pricing varies by plan, so check their site.

What Smart Table adds on top

Smart Table connects to your Square account through Square's own sign-in screen. From there it reads your menu and open cheques, and puts a guest layer over them:

  • QR ordering: guests scan the table code and order from your Square menu on their phone; orders write back to Square.
  • Pay at the table: guests review the itemized bill, tip and pay by card in the browser. No app download, no account.
  • Split bills and group pay: each guest pays their own share — equally, by item, or a custom amount — against the same cheque, which closes itself at zero.
  • A live floor view: staff see every table's state — seated, paying, paid — plus settlement metrics.
  • Review capture with a safety net: after paying, every guest is offered a public Google review and private feedback — no star pre-screen — and a low rating alerts your team instantly, while the guest is still seated.

Who each one is for

You need Square (or a POS like it) if you are opening, or your current till cannot keep up: the POS is the foundation everything stands on, and Smart Table is not one: it has no till or kitchen tickets of its own, and it only runs with Square.

You add Smart Table if Square already runs your room fine, but the last ten minutes leak time: tables waiting on the bill, servers sprinting cards back and forth, split bills stalling a section on a Friday. That bottleneck is what the guest layer removes — six tables can settle in the same minute because none of them is waiting for a shared device or a free server.

You keep both if — like most rooms — your guests are a mix: some want to scan, split and go; some want their server to handle everything.

Frequently asked questions

Does Smart Table replace Square?

No. Smart Table is a guest layer that runs on top of Square, not a point of sale. Square stays your system of record for menus, open cheques, payments, reports and payouts. Smart Table reads open cheques from Square and writes guest orders and payments back — the two are used together, not instead of each other.

How do payments work — through Smart Table or Square?

Through your Square account. You connect Smart Table through Square's sign-in screen, guests pay by card in their phone browser, and each payment is recorded in Square like a terminal payment, settling to your merchant account. No second processor, no separate payout stream.

What does the combination cost?

Smart Table pricing is in CAD per location: Starter is $49.99 per month or 1% per card payment; Base is $99 per month or 2%. Square's pricing varies by plan — check their site. Card processing stays with Square either way, and the Founding Pilot runs Starter free for 30 days, no card required.

What do guests need to use Smart Table?

Just a phone camera and a browser — no app download and no account. Guests who prefer the usual route are unaffected: staff can still take orders in person, run cards on your Square terminal and accept cash as today. Smart Table is an option layered over your normal process, never a replacement for it.
