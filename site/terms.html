1. The service

Smart Table is a pay-at-the-table platform for restaurants. Each table has a QR code; guests scan it with their phone to view their check from the restaurant’s point-of-sale (POS) system, split and pay the bill by card in their browser, and optionally order from the POS menu, request a waiter, receive an emailed receipt, leave feedback, and join a restaurant’s loyalty program where the restaurant enables it. Payments are recorded back to the POS, and restaurant staff manage tables, payments and service from a live web portal.

The platform works with Square POS today. Additional POS integrations may be added over time. A tabletop device is planned; it is not yet available, and devices, when offered, will be sold separately on the terms presented at that time.

3. Plans and fees

Each location is billed on one of two plans, selected in the portal. Prices are in Canadian dollars, before applicable taxes.

  • Commission plan — 2% per payment, no monthly fee. We charge 2% of each guest card payment processed through Smart Table, calculated on the total the guest pays, including tip. Where supported, the fee is collected by Square at the time of the payment; otherwise it accrues and is charged to your card on file when accrued fees reach $50, or when the oldest accrued fee is 25 days old, whichever comes first. Cash payments recorded by staff carry no fee.
  • Base subscription — $99 per month per location. A flat monthly fee charged to your card on file through Square. While a paid subscription period is running, no per-payment commission applies.
  • Pro — $199 per month is shown on our website as coming soon. It is not currently offered, and nothing in these Terms sells it.

You can switch plans or end a subscription for future periods at any time in the portal. Switching from Base to Commission takes effect at the end of the paid subscription period, so you are never charged both ways for the same days. Fees already collected or charged for a period are not refundable except where the law requires it. Your billing history, current plan and how each fee was charged are shown in the portal. If a charge to your card on file fails, we may retry it and may suspend paid features until outstanding fees are paid.

Smart Table does not process cash: cash payments are recorded by your staff in the portal for tracking, and the money itself never passes through us.
