Owner demo FAQ

Why is the owner demo by request instead of open?

Two reasons: the demo runs on the real portal, so each login is set up deliberately; and knowing your POS and room size lets us make the walkthrough relevant to you. The guest experience needs none of that, so the guest demo stays open to everyone.

What will I see once I’m approved?

The complete owner portal on a sample restaurant (Sample Bistro) with realistic history: live floor, orders, reports, per-table revenue, devices, staff, billing and settings. It’s sample data only — no real restaurant’s information is ever in the demo.

Does it cost anything, or need a card?

No. The demo is free and no payment details are asked for — approval is an email with your login, nothing else.

Curious what your guests feel first?

The guest demo needs no approval — scan, split, pay and print, exactly as a diner would.
