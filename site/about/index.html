About Smart Table

QR ordering and pay-at-the-table software for independent restaurants — built and based in Coquitlam, British Columbia.

What Smart Table is

Smart Table lets restaurant guests order and pay from their own phone. A printed QR code sits on each table; guests scan it, see the menu and their itemized bill, split it the way the table wants — equally, by item, or with group pay — tip, pay, and choose a receipt. There is no app to download and no account to create: everything happens in the phone browser.

For the restaurant, nothing about the existing setup changes. Smart Table works with Square today: it reads the table's open cheque from your POS and records the payment back into it, so payouts, refunds and reports behave exactly like a counter payment, settling into your own merchant account. Staff watch every table's state live — who has ordered, who has paid, who is waiting — instead of shuttling between tables, the printer and a single card terminal.

How it works

1. The guest scans the QR code on their table.
2. They order, split and pay from their phone — no app download.
3. The payment lands in your Square account, and your team sees the table update live.

Built in Coquitlam, for independent restaurants

Smart Table is built and based in Coquitlam, British Columbia, and made for independent restaurants — the places where the owner is often also the host, the schedule and the repair crew. It is designed for Metro Vancouver rooms first: printed QR codes that cost almost nothing to start, pricing in Canadian dollars, and a guest page that works in English, Persian, Chinese, Hindi and Punjabi. Guests who prefer the card terminal keep it — Smart Table runs alongside your current setup, never instead of it.
