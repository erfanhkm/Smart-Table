Is any of this a real payment?

No. This page is a simulation with a sample restaurant (Sample Bistro, Table 5), and the “on your own phone” link runs in test mode — use the test card shown there. Nothing is charged.

Does the guest need an app or an account?

Neither. The QR code opens a web page; the guest pays with a card or wallet and is done. That is the whole point — zero friction at the moment the bill usually stalls.

What does the device itself do?

The STT-101 shows the table’s QR code, signals table state with its LED ring (blue: awaiting payment, amber: service requested, green: paid), takes the service call on its physical button, and prints the receipt when the guest chooses print. Menus, payment, receipts and ratings all live on the guest’s phone.
